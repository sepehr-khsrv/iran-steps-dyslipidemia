# Dyslipidaemia trends, Iran STEPS 2007-2021: Stage 2 results summary

Generated by `R/18_results_index.R` on 16 November 2026. Every number below is in `output/results_index.json`. Prevalence is directly standardised to the 2016 census population aged 25-64 and given with 95% CIs (logit). Standardisation uses survey::svystandardize, which treats standardisation as post-stratification: the linearised scores are residualised within the standardisation cells, rather than the standardised weights being treated as fixed. APC is the annual percentage change from a survey-weighted log-link (quasi-Poisson) model on pooled records, adjusted for age group, sex and area.

## Main results

- National: 79.3 (78.0, 80.4)% in 2007, 77.3 (75.4, 79.0)% in 2011, 76.8 (75.9, 77.6)% in 2016 and 78.1 (77.0, 79.1)% in 2021. Change 2007-2021: -1.2 (-2.7, 0.4) points. APC -0.13 (-0.26, 0.01)% (P = 0.061). Departure from a log-linear trend: P = 0.006.
- Women: 84.5 (83.0, 85.9)% in 2007 and 81.4 (80.1, 82.6)% in 2021 (APC -0.33 (-0.48, -0.18)%). Men: 74.1 (72.1, 75.9)% and 74.8 (73.1, 76.4)% (APC 0.14 (-0.09, 0.37)%). Year x sex interaction P = 0.001.
- Women's prevalence fell from 2011 to 2016 (-3.8 (-6.1, -1.5) points) and rose from 2016 to 2021 (2.5 (0.8, 4.1) points). Men changed little between 2016 and 2021 (0.2 (-1.9, 2.3)).
- Age: APC 25-34 0.19 (-0.10, 0.47)%; 35-44 -0.12 (-0.36, 0.12)%; 45-54 -0.26 (-0.49, -0.03)%; 55-64 -0.53 (-0.76, -0.30)%. Year x age interaction P = 0.003.
- Area: APC urban -0.16 (-0.32, 0.00)%, rural -0.02 (-0.25, 0.20)%. Year x area interaction P = 0.296.
- Components 2007 to 2021 (standardised): TC >= 200 33.5 (32.1, 34.9)% to 18.3 (17.4, 19.3)%; LDL-C >= 130 29.6 (28.3, 30.9)% to 14.4 (13.5, 15.3)%; TG >= 150 37.8 (36.4, 39.1)% to 38.4 (37.1, 39.7)%; low HDL-C 60.5 (59.1, 61.8)% to 68.9 (67.7, 70.0)%. TC and LDL-C fell while low HDL-C rose, so the composite changed little.
- Medication: 9.1 (8.5, 9.8)% (weighted) reported current lipid-lowering drugs in 2021 (h14a); 5.8 (5.4, 6.3)% reported a statin in 2016 (h19, proxy). Counting treated people as cases, 2016-2021 change: 1.6 (0.3, 3.0) points (composite alone 1.3 (0.0, 2.7)).
- Without the HDL-C criterion, the 2007-2021 change was -8.5 (-10.4, -6.6) points (APC -2.04 (-2.30, -1.79)%). The sex difference in trend persists (women minus men change -8.5 (-11.9, -5.0)).
- Provinces: estimated in 29 provinces in 2007 (Tehran includes Alborz; Kurdistan has no laboratory data), 31 in 2011, 30 in 2016 (Qom not surveyed) and 31 in 2021. Provincial APC ranged from -1.55 (-2.27, -0.82)% (Ilam) to 1.68 (1.11, 2.26)% (West Azerbaijan). 14 of 31 provincial APCs had P < 0.05 (no adjustment for multiplicity).

## Fasting check (2021)

Only 2021 records fasting (clock times of the last food and of blood sampling, free text; participants were told to fast 12 hours). 2007 and 2011 have no fasting item; the 2016 items (Q 1395 items 165-166) are not in the released data. In the 2021 analytic sample, 40.9 (39.5, 42.4)% (weighted) had fasted 8 to < 12 h and 52.1 (50.5, 53.6)% 12 to 20 h; 0.4 (0.3, 0.5)% had fasted 4 to < 8 h, 0.6 (0.4, 0.9)% had an implausible parsed duration, and 2.3 (1.9, 2.9)% and 3.7 (3.0, 4.6)% had unparsable or no times. Weighted median 12 h.

- Restricting to participants with a parsed fast of at least 8 h (n = 14,560) changed the 2021 standardised prevalence of TG >= 150 from 38.4 (37.1, 39.7)% to 38.5 (37.3, 39.8)% (difference 0.14 (-0.29, 0.57) points) and of the composite from 78.1 (77.0, 79.1)% to 77.8 (76.7, 78.9)% (difference -0.30 (-0.56, -0.03) points). Restricting to at least 12 h gave differences of 0.21 (-1.05, 1.46) (TG) and -0.48 (-1.54, 0.58) (composite) points. Non-fasting samples in 2021 do not explain the 2016 to 2021 rise in TG >= 150.
